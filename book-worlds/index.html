How to play

Left stick moves.

LASSO, STEAM, PULSE, and FIRELIGHT when you have them.

Move with WASD or the left stick. Attack swings the Trail Key. Space jumps. Shift dodges. G guards. Q casts magic. F is the lantern, or the reaction when it flashes. E or Enter uses what is in front of you. 1 is a tonic. 2 dust devil. 3 trail mend. 4 wagon toss. L locks on. V is lasso. X is steam. Z is pulse. H is firelight.
