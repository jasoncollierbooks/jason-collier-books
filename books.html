Jang and Tom: Wagon Masters

In the spring of 1867, two thoroughly unqualified Philadelphia debtors do the only sensible thing left: invent new names, print flashy handbills, and sell themselves as expert wagon-train guides. What follows is two thousand miles of pure frontier chaos, from a river crossing that becomes a floating circus to a bear hunt that turns the hunters into the hunted. Three books of misadventure in one, it is a comic Western about friendship, fraud, and the glorious art of failing upward all the way to the Pacific.

The First Pulse

Something older than stars is listening. Born in the first light of the universe, an ancient intelligence has ridden electromagnetic waves for billions of years, slipping through radio, code, and circuitry and subtly shaping the tools every civilization builds. Humanity’s breakthroughs are not accidents; they are invitations.
