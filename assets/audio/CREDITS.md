# Sharp Eye Search scene audio

Recordings under this folder are **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication) from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses), recorded by Joseph SARDIN unless a page names someone else. They were trimmed, loudness-matched, and (for the beds) given a short equal-power crossfade so they loop. A few were filtered, as noted. No attribution is required by the license; this note is here so the source stays checkable.

Nothing in this folder is all-rights-reserved, CC-BY-NC, or a commercial library.

Total encoded size is about 2.6 MB. The game loads only the clips for the picture you open.

## Made in the browser, not from a recording

These are original Web Audio synthesis in `ispy-audio.js`. No suitable CC0 recording turned up that matched the picture (a pitched duck was tried for the geese and dropped):

- Honky-tonk piano phrases (saloon pictures)
- Room tone under indoor scenes
- Airship engine hum and propeller thrum
- A little warmth under the campfire crackle
- Harness jingle
- Goose honks (the coach full of crated geese)
- Hawk cry (open country and the mountain wreck)
- Alpaca hum (the alpaca coach)
- The small “found it” chime
