How to play

Tap a viewpoint card to start a story. The Guide reads each scene aloud; the Voice key turns narration off and on. At the end of a scene, tap a choice and the story goes that way. Restart replays the story you're in from the top, and Home goes back to the story cards. Progress saves on this device.

Your goals

    One path takes about 5 minutes, and finishing everything takes about 1.5–2 hours.
