About the Author

Jason Collier

Jason Collier writes stories across science fiction, outdoor suspense, and comic Western adventure. His books include The First Pulse, a chilling sci-fi tale of an ancient signal woven through human progress; Old Man On The Mountain, a lean woods thriller; and Jang and Tom: Wagon Masters, a frontier comedy about two thoroughly unqualified guides failing upward to California.
