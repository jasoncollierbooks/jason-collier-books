Music and sound

Stories by Jason Collier. Our shows, audiobooks and games use music and sounds shared by generous people. Here's who made them.

Jang & Tom radio show

The Treasure of Miller's Ridge

Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0. Excerpts were trimmed, faded and level-adjusted.

  • "Hillbilly Swing" by Kevin MacLeod
  • "Fireflies and Stardust" by Kevin MacLeod
  • "Sneaky Snitch" by Kevin MacLeod
  • "Loping Sting" by Kevin MacLeod

Studio audience: laughter recordings "approx-800-laugh-1", "approx-800-laughter-only-1" and "approx-800-laughter-and-clapter-1" by lonemonk (Freesound, via Wikimedia Commons), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 3.0.

Sound effects: other laughs, applause and sound effects from Freesound.org contributors under Creative Commons 0 (CC0), plus public-domain recordings from Wikimedia Commons and the U.S. National Park Service. CC0 and public domain sounds need no credit, but we thank everyone who shared them.

Story: adapted from Jason Collier's own stories of two friends back home in Pine Knot.

Old Man On The Mountain audiobook

Field recordings: National Park Service, Yellowstone Sound Library (public domain; recordings by NPS and the MSU Acoustic Atlas), including elk, wolves, coyotes, ravens, fire, wind and axe work, and a wolf-howl recording by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (public domain, via Wikimedia Commons).

All music, the score, the creature and the other sound effects were made for this production.

I Spy

Scene sounds are CC0 recordings from BigSoundBank, recorded by Joseph Sardin, trimmed and looped for each picture. A few sounds (the saloon piano, airship hum, goose honks, hawk cry and the "found it" chime) are made right in your browser.
