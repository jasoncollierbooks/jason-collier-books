Old ManOn The Mountain

🎧 Scary campfire story. Best with headphones, in the dark.

Part 1: Into the High Country

0:00 / 12:21

The complete, unabridged story by Jason Collier, performed by a full cast of voices with sound design and a horror score, in six parts (66 minutes in all). Harlan Wade walks into the winter high country alone for one more elk hunt, and the mountain is not empty.

Credits

Story: Old Man On The Mountain by Jason Collier, read unabridged. The narrator reads the narration and the characters speak their own lines.

Voices: the narrator and Harlan's voice with Chatterbox TTS by Resemble AI (MIT); the other characters generated with the Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (Apache-2.0) via kokoro-onnx (MIT). Pictures: AI-generated illustrations made for this audiobook.

Field recordings: National Park Service, Yellowstone Sound Library (public domain; recordings by NPS & the MSU Acoustic Atlas), including elk, wolves, coyotes, ravens, fire, wind and axe work; and a wolf-howl recording by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (public domain, via Wikimedia Commons). All music, the score, the creature and the other sound effects were synthesized for this production.
