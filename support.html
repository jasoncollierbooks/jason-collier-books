Common questions

How do I put the app on my iPhone home screen?
  1. Open collierentertainment.com/app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The Collier icon will show up with your other apps.

How do I put the app on my Android home screen?
  1. Open collierentertainment.com/app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then confirm.
The audio won't play. What can I try?
  • Turn the volume up, and on iPhone check the ring/silent switch on the side.
  • Tap the play button once more. Phones only let sound start after a tap.
  • Check that the show or game's own sound or mute button isn't switched off.
  • If you have Bluetooth headphones or a speaker connected, the sound may be going there.
  • Still quiet? Close the page or app completely and open it again.
Does it work offline?

Partly. Once you've opened the app with a signal, its main screens and pictures are saved on your phone, so it opens without one. Shows, audiobooks, videos and games still need a connection unless they've been downloaded. Offline episode downloads are planned for the Pine Knot Club, which isn't open yet.

I'm seeing an old version. How do I get the newest one?
  • Close the page or app all the way, then open it again. It usually updates on the second open.
  • On a computer, hold Shift and press the reload button.
  • On iPhone: Settings, Safari (on newer iPhones, Settings, Apps, Safari), Advanced, Website Data, search for "collier" and delete it. On Android: Chrome, Settings, Site settings, All sites, collierentertainment.com, Clear and reset.
  • If you added the app to your home screen, you can also delete the icon and add it again.

Clearing site data also clears game saves and settings stored on that device.

How do I sign in? Do I need to?

You don't need to. Everything free on the site works without signing in. If you'd like to:

  1. Tap Sign in in the menu (in the app: Help & info, then Sign in).
  2. Type your email and tap Send me a sign-in link.
  3. Open the email and tap Sign in. Use the same phone or computer.

No email? Check your spam folder and wait a minute. Links work once and expire after 15 minutes, so if one fails, just ask for a new one. To sign out or delete your account, go back to the Sign in page.

Where do I buy the books?

The books are on Amazon. See the Books page for each one.
